Conversion-Focused
Website Redesign

Transforming an outdated corporate site into a high-converting, data-driven experience that increased organic traffic by 42% and cut bounce rate by 22%.

Client
UNCOMN
Role
UX Design & Analytics
Timeline
4 Months
Industry
IT Consulting
The Challenge

An outdated site that couldn't keep up

UNCOMN's site hadn't been touched in years. Navigation was confusing and the service pages never said what the company actually did for you. There was also no analytics at all, so nobody could tell which parts were failing.

The Solution

Rebuilt it around what people actually needed

I restructured the sitemap, rewrote every service page, and rebuilt the forms around lead capture. Then I put in the analytics that should have been there all along: GA4, GTM, Consent Mode and Hotjar.

Process

How I got there

01 — Discovery

Stakeholder interviews, a competitive audit, and a full content inventory to find where people were dropping off.

02 — Architecture

Wireframes and components built around lead generation, with CTAs where people would actually reach them.

03 — Build

Full rebuild in Elementor Pro, responsive, with page speed handled as I went rather than afterwards.

04 — Measure

GA4 and GTM, VWO wired up for testing, Hotjar heatmaps, then cross-browser QA.
