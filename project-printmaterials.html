The Challenge

Digital brand, no physical presence

The digital brand was solid. Print didn't exist as a system, so every department made their own, with whatever fonts and colors they had. At career fairs the company looked like several different companies.

The Solution

Extended the digital system into print

Retractable banners, tabletop signage, one-pager templates and recruitment flyers, all pulling from the existing digital brand system so nothing had to be reinvented.

Process

How I got there

01 — Align

Talked to each department about what they actually needed at events.

02 — Design

Layouts in Illustrator and InDesign against the existing brand.

03 — Proof

Checked dimensions, bleed and vendor compatibility, then proofed for color.

04 — Deliver

Press-ready PDFs plus lighter digital versions for email.
